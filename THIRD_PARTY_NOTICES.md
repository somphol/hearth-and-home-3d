## Audio

No recorded audio assets are included. All fire, wind, ocean, crackle, and chime sounds are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.

## Generated assets

All textures (stone, wood, rug, paper, window, clouds, balloon fabric) and all 3D geometry are generated procedurally in the browser. No stock imagery is bundled.
